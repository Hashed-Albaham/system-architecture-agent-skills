# Architecture Decision Record

Use one record per consequential architecture choice. Keep it concise and revisit it when stated triggers occur.

## Title and status

- Decision:
- Status: proposed / accepted / superseded
- Date or version:
- Owner/reviewer:

## Context and requirements

What problem and measurable requirement does this address? Record constraints, relevant workload, invariants, assumptions, and what is not being decided.

## Options considered

For each viable option, state fit, consistency/failure semantics, cost, operational burden, security/privacy implications, and migration/reversal difficulty.

## Decision and consequences

State the selected option and why it fits better under the recorded assumptions. Include expected benefits, trade-offs, new failure modes, dependencies, and known limitations.

## Verification and revisit triggers

List official documentation, calculations, experiments, tests, load/failure drills, or human reviews required. State evidence and results separately. Define observable triggers that should reopen the decision, such as traffic thresholds, SLO misses, regulatory change, vendor behavior change, or operational capacity.
